### 💡 Who I Am
I am a multidisciplinary engineer dedicated to crafting **intelligent automated solutions** and bridging the gap between complex data and actionable insights. With a robust academic background in Chemical Engineering and hands-on expertise in **Data Science, Machine Learning, and Data Mining**, I build scalable AI pipelines and predictive models. Additionally, I am deeply involved in the **Web3 ecosystem** and algorithmic financial trading, developing precision-based tools for global markets.

### 🎯 My Expertise

* 🤖 **AI, Machine Learning & Data Mining** – Developing predictive models (ANN, SVM, Random Forest), conducting exploratory data analysis (EDA), and implementing MLOps architectures. I strictly apply the **CRISP-DM methodology** for data drift analysis and real-world deployment.
* 📈 **Quantitative Trading & Financial Engineering** – Building custom indicators via **Pine Script** and Expert Advisors (EAs) using **MQL4/MQL5**. Specialized in institutional methodologies including SMC, ICT Concepts, RTM, and Wyckoff Theory.
* ⛓️ **Web3, DeFi & Blockchain** – Creating smart contracts/tokens (ERC-20, BEP-20, SPL), designing advanced tokenomics, and integrating decentralized applications (DApps) with Web3 tools.
* 💻 **Web Application & UI/UX Design** – Crafting responsive, mobile-first web applications and user-centric interfaces using modern front-end technologies and design systems.
* ⚙️ **Process Simulation & Digital Twins** – Utilizing multiphysics modeling to develop Al-driven digital twins and intelligent dashboards for industrial processes (e.g., EAF Carbon Management).
